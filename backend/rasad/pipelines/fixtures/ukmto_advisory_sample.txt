UKMTO ADVISORY 901-26
Date: 2026-10-14
Subject: Incident, southern Red Sea

At 0412 UTC on 14 October 2026 UKMTO received a report of a vessel being struck by an unknown projectile approximately 42 NM SW of Al Hudaydah. The Master reports an explosion in close proximity to the vessel and minor damage; all crew are safe and the vessel is proceeding to her next port of call. Position 14 12.3 N, 042 31.0 E.

Vessels transiting the area are advised to exercise caution and report any suspicious activity to UKMTO.
